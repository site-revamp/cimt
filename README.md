CIMT UK Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
CIMT UK provides specialist, evidence-based Constraint Induced Movement Therapy (CIMT) for children and adults with hemiplegia and other neurological conditions. Its rehabilitation programmes focus on improving the functional use of the affected arm and hand through intensive therapy delivered by specialist physiotherapists and occupational therapists.

The redesigned homepage focuses on making CIMT's specialist services easier to understand, creating clearer journeys for parents, adult patients and referring professionals, and presenting essential treatment and programme information more effectively.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Visitor Journeys and Navigation
- Created distinct pathways for parents, adult patients and referring professionals.
- Improved the organisation of treatment information to help each audience find relevant services.
- Made the next steps towards enquiring about therapy easier to identify.
Problem addressed: The previous homepage lacked clearly separated visitor journeys, making it difficult for different audiences to find relevant information.
2. More Reliable Results and Statistics Presentation
- Addressed the presentation of performance statistics displaying incorrect or incomplete values such as 0% and 0+.
- Prioritised accurate, verified information rather than introducing unsupported performance figures.
- Improved the presentation of numerical information to avoid misleading visitors.
Problem addressed: Incorrect or unloaded statistics could undermine the credibility of a specialist rehabilitation provider.
3. Better Structured Treatment and Booking Information
- Improved the presentation of programme duration, daily therapy sessions and treatment information.
- Made eligibility and programme details easier to follow.
- Structured the enquiry and booking journey to help visitors understand how to get started.
Problem addressed: Important treatment and booking information was previously distributed across multiple pages, making the decision-making process less straightforward.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: Established a clearer, more professional appearance appropriate for a specialist neurological rehabilitation provider.
- Homepage Layout: Improved content hierarchy and section organisation, giving key services and patient pathways greater prominence.
- User Experience: Simplified navigation for parents, adult patients and referring professionals.
- Content Consistency: Presented programme details, treatment information and supporting statistics in a more structured format.
- Trust and Credibility: Emphasised specialist expertise, evidence-based rehabilitation and accurate information without relying on unverified results.

Project Focus: Clearer patient journeys, credible information presentation and improved access to specialist rehabilitation and programme details.
