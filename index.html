Understanding suitability

Is CIMT the right approach?

Eligibility depends on the individual's presentation, abilities and capacity to participate in intensive therapy. A specialist therapist must assess suitability.

For Children

CIMT's published programme information includes children aged 18 months and above with one affected upper limb.

  • One weaker and one unaffected arm.
  • Some active movement in the affected wrist and fingers.
  • Ability to participate in age-appropriate therapy activities.
  • Individual assessment by a specialist CIMT therapist.
Children's Suitability ↗

For Adults

Adult programmes are intended for people with upper-limb weakness associated with neurological damage.

  • One affected upper limb.
  • Some active wrist and finger movement.
  • Generally at least six months after neurological injury.
  • Ability to engage in intensive practice and assessment.

Helpful information

Questions before getting started?

A few useful answers to help you understand the programme and what to expect.

Ask the CIMT Team ↗
What does CIMT stand for?
CIMT stands for Constraint Induced Movement Therapy. It is a specialist rehabilitation approach focused on increasing use of the weaker arm and hand.
How long do CIMT programmes last?
CIMT's published programme structure is generally three to four weeks for children and two to three weeks for adults. The therapist recommends the appropriate duration following assessment.
How intensive are the therapy sessions?
Published programme information describes sessions lasting approximately three hours a day, five days a week. The exact programme depends on the patient group.
Can people travel from outside Manchester or Liverpool?
Yes. CIMT states that patients travel from across the UK and beyond. The team can provide local information and accommodation suggestions.
How do I find out whether CIMT is suitable?
The first step is a free telephone consultation with a CIMT therapist. The therapist will discuss the condition, current abilities and possible next steps.
Do I have to book a programme after the consultation?
No. The consultation provides an opportunity to ask questions and discuss suitability. You are not obliged to proceed with a booking.
